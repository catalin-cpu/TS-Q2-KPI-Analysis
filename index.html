Q2 to date — analysis (MRR & EBITDA + MRR movement)
MRR
MoM growth is building. −0.6% (Feb) → +1.0% → +0.6% → +1.2% (May, best month of 2026); exits Q2 TD at $242.5k, +1.8% vs Q1 end.
New MRR
Rising despite fewer G4s. $2.9k → $3.5k/mo (+21%) while new logos slipped 170 → 166/mo — ARPU of new users up ~25% ($16.7 → $20.9).
Upgrades
Oscillating but growing. $2.1k–$2.8k monthly range; Q2 avg $2.7k/mo, +18% vs Q1.
Retention
Retention efforts are paying. Reactivation MRR trending up ($768 → $1,692/mo; May $2.4k is the year high) and downgrades trending down (May −$942, year low).
Churn
Gross churn MRR oscillating and growing vs Q1. −$3.0k / −$5.0k / −$3.3k / −$5.1k / −$4.1k monthly; Q2 avg −$4.6k vs Q1 −$3.8k (−21%).
Cost
EBITDA recovery is 95% cost-driven. Costs −$39.8k/mo vs Q1 ($230.4k → $190.6k); revenue nearly flat (+0.8%/mo).
Plan
Plan gap: EBITDA −14.2% vs MRR −5.7%. EBITDA TD $101.8k vs $118.6k plan; MRR $242.5k vs $257.1k plan — a ~6% MRR shortfall amplifies ~2.5× at EBITDA level. Costs run $8.8k below plan; the miss is all revenue.
Trend
Gap to plan narrowing. Margin vs plan: Apr −2.9pp (17.7% vs 20.6%) → May −1.5pp (24.5% vs 26.0%).

Traffic
The constraint is upstream. Web users (excl. guides) −13.8% vs Q1; May 29.3k is the 2026 low. Sign-up rate is steady (~2%) — fewer visitors, same capture.
Trials
Trial volume held despite −10.7% sign-ups. Trial-start rate jumped to 80.4% (May 86.1%, a record) — trials 522/mo vs 518 in Q1.
Volume
The real problem is G1 volume, and it’s accelerating. YoY: Jan −16% → Apr −33% → May −47%. Apr+May cohorts −40% vs 2025.
Conversion
Conversion is genuinely improving. Cohorted trial rate climbed every month of 2026 (63% → 74%) vs 64% avg in 2025 — better capture is partially masking the volume decline.
June setup
June G4s rebound from the May cohort. 450 May trials × ~34% mature rate ≈ 152 total; 72 landed → +70–86 arrive as June G4 events.
